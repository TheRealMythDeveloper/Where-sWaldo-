# Where's Waldo?

A userscript that randomly hides Waldo somewhere on the websites you visit. He might be hiding in a word, pretending to be an image, or just barely peeking out somewhere.

Good luck finding him.

**What is this?**

Where's Waldo? is a Tampermonkey userscript. It injects Waldo into webpages and gives him random hiding spots so you might find him while browsing.

He's supposed to be just a little hard to spot. 

**Features**

- Waldo
- Waldo can be words, or just himself.
- or images.

## Installation

1. Install [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/).
2. Create a new userscript.
3. Copy the contents of the script into it.
4. Save it.
5. Visit a website and try to find Waldo.

Or, Go here and install. [GreasyFork](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/606801-where-s-waldo)

**How to use**

Just browse normally.

Waldo will hide somewhere on the page. If you find him, click him. He'll let you know that you found him and then hide somewhere else.

He can be literally ANYWHERE. 
Even on parts of the page like the dropdown questions!

## Compatibility

The script works on regular webpages, but some websites may have layouts or scripts that interfere with his hiding spots. Certain pages may not have enough suitable text or images for him to hide in.
